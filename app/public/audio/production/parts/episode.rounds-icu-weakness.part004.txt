Now connect ICU nutrition & refeeding risk to Refeeding syndrome. Insulin-driven intracellular shifts after refeeding can rapidly lower phosphate, potassium and magnesium and precipitate cardiopulmonary or neurologic deterioration.

The relationship matters because Reintroducing carbohydrate after prolonged undernutrition raises insulin and shifts phosphate, potassium and magnesium intracellularly while increasing thiamine demand. At the bedside, identify high-risk patients before feeding escalation and monitor electrolytes and cardiopulmonary/neurologic response.

Now connect ICU nutrition & refeeding risk to ICU-acquired weakness & recovery. Critical illness, immobility, inflammation, sedation and organ failure can produce profound weakness and long-term functional loss.

The relationship matters because Inflammation, immobility, neuropathy, myopathy, sedation and undernutrition can cause profound ICU-acquired weakness and prolonged post-ICU disability. At the bedside, build mobility, delirium prevention, nutrition and sedation minimization into daily care rather than starting “rehab” only after organ failure resolves.
