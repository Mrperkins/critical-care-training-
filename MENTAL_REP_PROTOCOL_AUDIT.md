# Mental Rep Protocol-Grade Audit

Updated: 2026-10-04

## Purpose

Every existing Mental Rep has been re-reviewed against the new **protocol-grade narration** standard, and new reps must meet that standard on first entry.

A rep passes only when a learner can mentally rehearse the physical sequence without having to supply missing procedural knowledge from memory. "Hands-first" is necessary but not sufficient. Anatomical, device and workflow shorthand must be unpacked inside the narration itself.

## Required narration contract

For invasive, device-dependent or anatomy-dependent work, the script must explicitly establish:

1. starting patient/body/device position;
2. first unmistakable anchor;
3. physical route from anchor to target;
4. adjacent anatomy or hardware;
5. structures/states to avoid;
6. what proves the target or device state;
7. the next literal hand action;
8. stop/re-orient criteria;
9. a specific endpoint proving that the step worked.

A local protocol, manufacturer IFU or medical-direction dependency may control a dose, device setting or exact variant. That dependency is allowed, but it may not replace the physical choreography. The narration should still say how the provider finds the correct row/setting/component, verifies it and executes the step.

## Specific terminology rule

A script fails if it assumes a landmark term is self-explanatory.

Examples now enforced in current scripts:

- **Safe triangle:** lateral edge of pectoralis major anteriorly, anterior edge of latissimus dorsi posteriorly, apex below the axilla, lower boundary around the fifth-interspace level; the script also teaches how to reach the interspace by rib counting.
- **Pleural access / entry:** not merely "get into the pleural space." The script walks skin incision → blunt dissection → chest wall → superior border of the lower rib → controlled parietal-pleura entry → loss of resistance/air or fluid as appropriate → gloved-finger confirmation.
- **Phlebostatic axis:** fourth intercostal space at the mid-axillary line in the supine adult, used here as the common right-atrial-level reference for pressure transducers.
- **Tragus / EAM reference:** the cartilage immediately in front of the ear-canal opening / the opening itself, used in the supine neutral-head example to approximate the foramen-of-Monro horizontal level for EVD leveling.
- **Mid-axillary line:** vertical line descending from the middle of the axilla.
- **Posterior axillary line:** vertical line descending from the posterior axillary fold.
- **Xiphoid:** inferior tip of the sternum; "subxiphoid" means immediately inferior to that palpable landmark.
- **Tibial tuberosity:** firm anterior tibial prominence just below the knee where the patellar tendon inserts; adult proximal-tibial IO target is then built from that anchor for the device-specific example.

## New procedure reps that must use the same standard

These are not yet part of the current required set, but when authored they must start at this protocol-grade depth rather than receiving a later rewrite:

- pericardiocentesis;
- thoracentesis / pleural catheter placement;
- emergency transvenous pacing;
- temporary pacing-wire troubleshooting;
- dialysis-catheter troubleshooting;
- targeted burn and compartment-release procedures as appropriate to scope.

### Escharotomy implementation benchmark

The new escharotomy rep demonstrates the required depth. It does **not** say "identify the standard escharotomy incision line." It defines the constricting physiology, exposes the entire involved segment, establishes proximal/distal viable margins, maps the medial/lateral or chest release lines, names location-specific neurovascular danger zones, stops the release at subcutaneous fat rather than fascia, and requires immediate objective reassessment.

## Voice-production state

Narration changes in this audit intentionally do **not** block further engineering on external voice rendering.

Changed beats remain transcript-hash stale until a dedicated voice-materialization pass generates and imports matching durable audio. The production gate must continue to reject stale audio; the solution is to render the new narration, not to weaken the gate.

### Sequence-order gate

Protocol-grade CI now verifies not only that required landmarks/actions exist, but that each Mental Rep retains its intended beat order. This prevents correct steps from drifting into the wrong sequence—for example, arterial-system priming after puncture or IJ sterile setup after the needle has already entered.
