Goals of care & prognostic communication. High-quality critical care includes eliciting values, communicating uncertainty and aligning burdensome treatments with realistic goals.

Build the mechanism first. Goals-of-care decisions align treatments with the patient’s values and realistic outcomes. Prognosis is probabilistic and changes with response over time.

At the bedside, use best-case/worst-case/most-likely framing and time-limited trials with explicit endpoints when uncertainty remains.

Two traps to actively avoid: Presenting procedures as neutral menu choices without recommendation. And: Using false precision to avoid discussing uncertainty.

The integration move is this: A high-quality critical-care plan includes what outcome the patient would consider worth the burdens of treatment.

Keep these terms available in your mental model: surrogate decision-maker, time-limited trial, best case / worst case.
