Calm care, treatment, observation
Upper-airway narrowing may be important before oxygen saturation falls. Stridor at rest, recession, behavior and air entry guide severity assessment. Keep the child calm and minimize distressing interventions. Use corticosteroid and nebulized epinephrine when indicated by the clinical pathway. Observe for recurrence and escalate if response is inadequate or the presentation is atypical. The response over time matters. A treatment can reduce symptoms before we know whether that improvement will last, so the observation timeline belongs in the plan.
