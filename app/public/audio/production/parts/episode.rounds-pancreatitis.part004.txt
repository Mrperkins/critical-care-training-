ICU nutrition & refeeding risk. Nutrition in critical illness is a metabolic therapy with timing, route, protein, glycemic and electrolyte consequences.

Build the mechanism first. Critical illness changes protein turnover, insulin sensitivity and energy use. Nutrition cannot fully reverse catabolism but route, timing and protein strategy influence recovery.

At the bedside, prefer enteral feeding when feasible, assess aspiration/hemodynamic risk and monitor tolerance rather than chasing an exact calorie number immediately.

Two traps to actively avoid: Equating calories with adequate nutrition. And: Overfeeding unstable patients or missing refeeding risk.
