The relationship matters because Oxygen delivery equals cardiac output multiplied by arterial oxygen content, which is dominated by hemoglobin-bound oxygen rather than dissolved oxygen. At the bedside, normal saturation can coexist with critically low delivery in severe anemia or low-flow shock.
