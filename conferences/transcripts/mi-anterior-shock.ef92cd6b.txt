Reperfuse and support the failing circulation
Congestion and poor forward flow can coexist. Acute infarction with shock requires immediate reperfusion planning while supporting oxygenation and circulation. Activate urgent coronary and cardiogenic-shock care with the receiving team. Support oxygenation and perfusion with attention to the hemodynamic effects of respiratory interventions.
