Myxedema coma. Severe hypothyroidism can produce hypothermia, bradycardia, hypoventilation, hyponatremia, hypotension and altered consciousness.

Build the mechanism first. Extreme hypothyroidism reduces metabolic rate, ventilatory drive and cardiac performance while impairing free-water handling and thermoregulation.

At the bedside, anticipate hypoventilation, bradycardia, hypotension, hyponatremia and hypothermia; consider adrenal coverage while treating.

Two traps to actively avoid: Aggressive external rewarming that worsens vasodilation. And: Missing ventilatory failure because oxygen saturation appears acceptable.

The integration move is this: The syndrome is global metabolic slowing with limited cardiovascular and respiratory reserve.
