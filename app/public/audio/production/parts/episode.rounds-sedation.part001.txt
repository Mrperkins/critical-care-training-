ICU analgesia, sedation & synchrony. Sedation strategy balances analgesia, comfort, ventilator synchrony, neurologic assessment, hemodynamics and delirium risk.

Build the mechanism first. Analgesics and sedatives alter pain, awareness, respiratory drive, vascular tone and neurologic assessment. Deep sedation is a physiologic intervention with downstream consequences.

At the bedside, separate pain, anxiety, delirium, dyssynchrony and hypoxemia before escalating sedation; use an explicit target and reassess it.

Two traps to actively avoid: Using paralysis as evidence of comfort. And: Treating agitation as a medication deficiency without finding the cause.

The integration move is this: Sedation strategy should serve a clinical goal, not simply silence movement.

Keep these terms available in your mental model: analgosedation, RASS, daily awakening.
