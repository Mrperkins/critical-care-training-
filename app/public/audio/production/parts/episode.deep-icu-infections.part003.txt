Now: Empiric breadth without permanent breadth.

Antimicrobial resistance & stewardship. Empiric antimicrobial breadth should reflect illness severity, host factors and local resistance, then narrow as evidence improves.

Build the mechanism first. Resistance emerges from organism biology plus selective pressure; empiric breadth should match host/severity/local epidemiology and then contract as evidence accumulates.

At the bedside, use prior cultures, recent exposure, local antibiogram and syndrome to choose empiric therapy, then deliberately de-escalate or stop when justified.

Two traps to actively avoid: Equating “sicker” with unlimited antimicrobial breadth. And: Failing to reassess antibiotics after cultures and source data return.

The integration move is this: Stewardship is not undertreatment; it is repeated matching of therapy to the probability landscape.

Keep these terms available in your mental model: de-escalation, antibiogram, MDR.
