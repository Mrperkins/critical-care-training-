# Narration clips

One clip per spoken line, named by its id (`app/public/vo/narration.json` records each clip's hash). Rendered offline
by the natural neural narrator — **Kokoro-82M v1.0** (Apache-2.0, hexgrad/Kokoro-82M; full-precision ONNX build from
thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx), voice `af_heart` — from this app's own lesson text. Audio-app clips are in
`audio/narration/`. See `app/README.md` › Narration to re-render after editing any spoken text.
