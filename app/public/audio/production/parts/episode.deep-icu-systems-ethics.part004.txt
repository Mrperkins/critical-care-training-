Now: Donation after ethical separation.

Organ donation & donor physiology. Organ donation requires separation of death determination from donation decisions and meticulous donor physiology after authorization.

Build the mechanism first. Donation decisions occur only after appropriate death determination pathways and authorization; donor physiology can then require intensive hemodynamic, endocrine and respiratory management.

At the bedside, maintain strict separation between death determination and donation while supporting organs according to established donor-management processes.

Two traps to actively avoid: Allowing donation considerations to influence death determination. And: Assuming critical-care work ends once death by neurologic criteria is established.

The integration move is this: Ethical separation and excellent physiology management are both required.

Keep these terms available in your mental model: donor management, organ procurement organization.

What expert-level use of this concept looks like is this: Maintain ethical separation between death determination and donation. Recognize donor hemodynamic/endocrine physiology as active critical care.
