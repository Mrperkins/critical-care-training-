Now connect Right ventricular failure to Pulmonary vascular resistance. Resistance faced by the RV, influenced by lung volume, oxygenation, CO2, pH, pulmonary vascular disease and mechanical ventilation.

The relationship matters because Pulmonary vascular resistance reflects the combined behavior of alveolar and extra-alveolar vessels and is affected by lung volume, oxygen, CO2, pH and pulmonary vascular disease. At the bedside, both severe derecruitment and overdistension can raise RV afterload. Hypoxemia, hypercapnia and acidosis can compound the problem.

Now connect Right ventricular failure to Ventricular interdependence. The ventricles share a septum and constrained pericardial space, so pressure or volume loading of one changes filling and geometry of the other.

The relationship matters because The ventricles share a septum and a constrained pericardial space. Acute RV pressure or volume loading can shift the septum and reduce LV diastolic filling. At the bedside, a falling LV stroke volume in RV failure may occur despite apparently adequate intravascular volume because geometry, not supply, is limiting filling.

Now: Dead space and the gas-exchange clues.

Dead space. Ventilation that does not effectively participate in CO2 exchange, from conducting airways or ventilated but poorly perfused alveoli.

Build the mechanism first. Dead-space ventilation reaches regions that do not effectively eliminate CO2 because perfusion is absent or inadequate, or because gas remains in conducting airways.

At the bedside, rising minute-ventilation requirement, widening PaCO2–EtCO2 gap or unexpectedly low EtCO2 can signal increasing dead space when ventilation is otherwise unchanged.

Two traps to actively avoid: Equating all hypercapnia with low minute ventilation. And: Using EtCO2 as a fixed conversion to PaCO2 in shock.

The integration move is this: CO2 is a ventilation-perfusion signal as well as a ventilation signal.

Keep these terms available in your mental model: VD/VT, alveolar dead space.

What expert-level use of this concept looks like is this: Use PaCO2–EtCO2 behavior and minute ventilation requirements to reason about changing dead space.
