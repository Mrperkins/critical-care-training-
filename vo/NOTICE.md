# Narration clips

Clips named after lesson cues and steps. The original step-lesson clips were rendered with an offline neural voice
when the site was first published. Clips added from September 2026 (Director lessons, the Lines step lessons, the
special-population lessons) were rendered with **Kokoro-82M** (Apache-2.0, hexgrad/Kokoro-82M; ONNX build from
thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx), voice `bf_emma`, from this app's own lesson text. See `app/README.md` › Narration.
