Now: The vent becomes part of the circulation.

peep. Positive end-expiratory pressure used to preserve recruitment and oxygenation, with important mechanical and hemodynamic effects.

Build the mechanism first. peep can prevent end-expiratory collapse and recruit unstable lung units, but can also overdistend already-open units and transmit pressure to the thorax.

At the bedside, judge a peep change by oxygenation, compliance, driving pressure, hemodynamics and the disease’s recruitability rather than by oxygen saturation alone.

Two traps to actively avoid: Chasing oxygenation while ignoring falling cardiac output. And: Assuming higher peep is either universally protective or universally harmful.

The integration move is this: The useful peep is the one that improves the whole patient, not just the alveolar oxygen number.
