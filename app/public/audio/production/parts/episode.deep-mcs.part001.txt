Now connect Mechanical circulatory support to Intra-aortic balloon pump physiology. I A B P timing uses diastolic augmentation and presystolic deflation to alter coronary perfusion and LV afterload.

The relationship matters because I A B P inflates in diastole to augment aortic diastolic pressure and deflates before systole to reduce LV afterload. Benefit depends on timing and native rhythm/hemodynamics. At the bedside, use the arterial waveform to judge inflation near the dicrotic notch and presystolic deflation; compare assisted and unassisted beats.

Now connect Mechanical circulatory support to E C M O circuit & physiology literacy. Extracorporeal support changes preload, afterload, gas exchange and oxygen delivery through a circuit whose configuration determines what it can support.

The relationship matters because E C M O drains venous blood, pumps it through a membrane lung and returns it to the circulation. VV supports gas exchange; VA additionally supports systemic flow and changes cardiac loading conditions. At the bedside, follow blood through drainage, pump, oxygenator and return while considering native heart/lung function and cannula position.
