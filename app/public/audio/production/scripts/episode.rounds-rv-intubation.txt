You’re at the bedside. The blood pressure has fallen after intubation. Before you reach for fluid, build the physiology in your head. Picture the right ventricle pushing against the pulmonary circulation. Positive pressure has changed venous return, pulmonary vascular resistance, and the pressure surrounding the heart. Your job is not to chase a number. Your job is to decide what changed, predict what your intervention should do, and then watch whether the patient proves your model right.
